This Week 2 report simulates laboratory experiment procedures and data collection methods, and documents a mock experiment including all the necessary laboratory procedures relevant to biotechnology research, covering every requirement of the task. The hypothetical biotechnological process chosen is the production and quantification of polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) in Cupriavidus necator growing on a volatile fatty acid feed, which continues directly from the experimental design proposed in the previous component so that the internship forms a coherent programme. The document is organised as a full laboratory procedure across five parts: cryostock revival and a two-stage preculture; shake-flask cultivation with timed sampling every four hours for forty-eight hours; harvest, lyophilisation and gravimetric cell dry weight determination; acidic methanolysis with GC-FID quantification; and Nile red flow cytometry as an independent cross-check. Every step is stated with its reagent quantities, equipment settings and timing, and each part ends in an acceptance gate that must pass before the next begins. The reagent and media section gives complete recipes, including the mineral medium composition, the trace element and vitamin solutions, and the preparation of the VFA feed, with the rule that acid is added to methanol and never the reverse. The safety section classifies every hazardous substance by its GHS hazard statements and applies the standard hierarchy of controls in order, from elimination through engineering and administrative controls to personal protective equipment. The data collection strategy specifies what is measured, with which instrument, how often and in what form, together with a worked example of the full calculation chain from raw peak areas to a reported titre. The quality control section sets out the controls run with every batch, the pre-specified acceptance criteria, the investigation path when a criterion fails, and a carbon-balance check that is independent of both analytical methods. Five figures include the required workflow flowchart.
