## Summary

A five-part laboratory procedure from cryostock revival through two-stage preculture, fed-batch cultivation, harvest and lyophilisation, acidic methanolysis with GC-FID, and Nile red flow cytometry as an independent cross-check. Its most consequential output was not a procedure but a number: VFA inhibition sets in above roughly 5 g/L. That figure became the design constraint the prototype is built around.
