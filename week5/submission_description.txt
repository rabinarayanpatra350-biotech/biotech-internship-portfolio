This Week 5 report integrates all previous work into an in-depth final report encapsulating the research, experimental design, data analysis and prototype development carried out across the internship, and it is submitted alongside the presentation slide deck required by the task, covering every requirement. The final report consolidates the four earlier components into a cohesive narrative of one problem and one proposed answer to it. The problem is that polyhydroxyalkanoate bioplastic is a good material that cannot compete on price, because the carbon feedstock accounts for up to half of what it costs to make; the answer is a process that takes its carbon from food waste and a modular unit that runs that process where the waste is generated. Section 3 states the methodology used for integration explicitly: each component was carried out as a self-contained investigation, integration was performed by following the dependencies each component placed on the others, and where a later component found an earlier one incomplete or wrong the discrepancy was reported rather than smoothed over. The report traces ten of the prototype's design decisions to the specific findings in earlier components that constrain them, showing how the parts complement each other, and it documents the dependencies running in both directions, including the case where the prototype's cost analysis improved the first component's reasoning. A critical analysis section sets out what the project established, what it left uncertain, and what went wrong, including a full account of a multiple-sequence alignment method that was found to be broken by a validation test and was replaced, together with the lessons generalised from it. A final section covers future research questions, product development potential and an honest assessment of readiness. The accompanying presentation is a fourteen-slide briefing for a research panel, supplied as both PPTX and PDF, with every slide carrying speaker notes, headline claims, supporting visuals and bullet-point summaries, and its outline is reproduced in the report so that both deliverables read as one submission.
