# Week 1 — Experimental Design and Literature Review

**Question it answered:** Can the product work, and what constrains it?
**Repository:** [`biotech-week1-experimental-design`](https://github.com/rabinarayanpatra350-biotech/biotech-week1-experimental-design)
**Deliverable:** `Experimental_Design_and_Literature_Review_Biotech.docx` (and `.pdf`)

## Summary

A fourteen-source literature review establishing that PHA costs US$4-6 per kg against US$1.1-1.4 for the petrochemical plastics it must displace, with the carbon feedstock accounting for up to half of production cost. Concluded that PHA is a feedstock problem rather than a polymer-chemistry problem, chose food-waste volatile fatty acids as the feedstock and Cupriavidus necator as the chassis, and identified three specific gaps.
