# Week 4 — Prototype Development and Findings Documentation

## Summary

A conceptual modular bioprocess unit converting food waste into PHA at the point of generation, with four modules, a pH-static control loop holding the reactor at 1 g/L against the 5 g/L threshold from Week 2, a projected output of 1.13 kg/day of dry polymer from 20 kg/day of dry waste, twelve scored failure modes and a four-stage gated development plan.
