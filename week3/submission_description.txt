This Week 3 report documents a complete bioinformatics data analysis performed on a genomic protein dataset and interprets the result for a biotechnological product question, covering every requirement of the task. The dataset is the family of polyhydroxyalkanoate (PHA) synthases, the enzymes that make biodegradable bioplastics, sampled as eighteen sequences spanning the four recognised PhaC enzyme classes plus an archaeal outgroup and retrieved through the UniProt REST API so that the dataset is fully reproducible. The analysis follows a complete pipeline. Data cleaning applied a pre-specified rule that removes any sequence shorter than eighty per cent of the median length of its own enzyme class, removing two truncated entries and retaining sixteen. A reference-guided multiple sequence alignment produced 676 columns, trimmed to 562 at fifty per cent occupancy or above. The alignment was then validated against an independent distance estimator, and this step proved decisive: the first alignment method implemented produced distances correlating minus 0.16 with direct pairwise distances and inverted the taxonomic signal entirely, so it was replaced, and the final alignment passes the same test at a correlation of 0.942. The phylogeny, reconstructed by neighbour joining with a hundred-replicate bootstrap, recovers every one of the five sequence groups as a monophyletic clade in one hundred per cent of replicates, and the identity heatmap shows the class blocks cleanly, with within-class identity at 46 per cent against 22 per cent between classes. Conservation analysis identifies fifteen invariant columns and twenty-two at or above ninety per cent identity, including an invariant catalytic cysteine. The most useful finding is a class-specific active-site signature: each PhaC class carries a different variant of the lipase-box motif, which controls substrate chain length, so an uncharacterised synthase can be assigned a probable product spectrum from seven residues of sequence. Six figures include a phylogenetic tree and an identity heatmap, and the report connects the findings directly to the PHA production project developed in the earlier components.
