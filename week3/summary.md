# Week 3 — Bioinformatics Data Analysis and Interpretation

**Question it answered:** Which enzyme, and how do we know?
**Repository:** [`biotech-week3-bioinformatics`](https://github.com/rabinarayanpatra350-biotech/biotech-week3-bioinformatics)
**Deliverable:** `Bioinformatics_Analysis_and_Interpretation_Biotech.docx` (and `.pdf`)

## Summary

A complete bioinformatics pipeline on eighteen real PHA synthase sequences across four enzyme classes and an archaeal outgroup: cleaning, reference-guided alignment, validation of that alignment, neighbour-joining phylogeny with 100-replicate bootstrap, conservation analysis and blind motif discovery. Found a class-specific lipase-box signature that predicts substrate range from seven residues of sequence. The first alignment method failed validation and was replaced.
